From Software has taken the refined combat mechanics of Dark Souls and successfully built a captivating open world around them.

## World Design
The Lands Between reward curiosity at every turn. Secrets, dungeons, and world bosses are strategically placed without relying on clutter.

## Verdict
Elden Ring is a generation-defining achievement that demands your attention.
